## Notes

### Where a paid tool is clearly better, and why

| Stage | Paid pick | Why it beats the free option |
|---|---|---|
| Transcription | Scribe v2 | Open options either normalize away fillers (Whisper-family) or ship non-commercial weights (CrisperWhisper standard). |
| Video understanding | Gemini Watcher | Gemini is the only frontier family that both watches and hears video, and the open Omni models trail it badly. |
| Music and SFX | Epidemic Sound | Sublicensing plus Content ID safelisting, beat data and exact-length versions. No free library offers any of this. |
| B-roll | Premium stock (Shutterstock) | Vertical filter, 4K and per-asset licences. |
| AI b-roll | Omni Flash, Veo, GPT Image | They top the leaderboards, and the best open models carry non-commercial or territory restrictions. |

The commercial voice tools (ElevenLabs Voice Isolator, ai-coustics, Auphonic) and the Olign aligner are **not** clearly better. No independent benchmark shows it, so they enter only as best-of-N candidates that must pass the metric gates.

### Speed changes

Use **Rubber Band v4, R3 "Finer" engine** through FFmpeg's `rubberband` filter on the server (GPL-2.0-or-later, commercial licence available). A/B it against Signalsmith Stretch (MIT). Limits:
- At most about 1.1×, with a hard cap of 1.2×.
- Only for slow speakers.
- Never on the hook, punchlines or instructions.

For the iOS editor preview, use AVAudioUnitTimePitch or Signalsmith rather than Rubber Band, because GPL code can't ship in the app.

### What we actually build (everything else above is off the shelf)

1. The layered take index and its query tools (`get_words`, `get_gaps`, `get_prosody`, `view_frames`, `judge_clip`, `redecode`).
2. The acoustic cut-edge snapper and the crop-path smoother.
3. A typed JSON timeline compiled into:
   - a frame-quantized Remotion render plan;
   - a sample-accurate FFmpeg audio graph;
   - an iOS preview description.
4. The caption placer and validator (safe zones and face collisions).
5. Conform and gate orchestration.
6. Human-written craft skills: principles with ranges, each range labelled with its evidence tier.

### License risk flags for commercial use

**1. Exclude: non-commercial or restricted weights**
- CrisperWhisper standard weights (Pro requires a commercial licence).
- MMS and ctc-forced-aligner default weights.
- Rev Reverb weights (licence "other").
- NISQA, DOVER, MatAnyone/MatAnyone 2, InsightFace models.
- madmom models, MusicGen, MMAudio.
- Depth Anything V2 Base, Large and Giant.
- Qwen-Image-2.1, FLUX.2 [dev] and klein 9B weights.
- FA-Bench harness (PolyForm Noncommercial).
- whisper-timestamped: AGPL, whose network clause applies to a server.

**2. Territory or scale limits**
- MiniMax H3 weights: excludes the US, including outputs.
- HunyuanVideo 1.5 and HunyuanVideo-Foley: exclude the EU, UK and South Korea; need a licence above 100M MAU; outputs can't train other models.
- Kling: written permission required for commercial use.
- Stable Audio 3: free only under $1M revenue.
- LTX-2: free only under $10M ARR.

**3. GPL: fine server-side, never in the iOS binary**
- Parselmouth, pedalboard, color-matcher, Rubber Band, x264/x265, FFmpeg GPL builds.
- A libfdk_aac build is "nonfree" and must never be redistributed.

**4. Source-available with fees**
- Remotion (Automators tier).

**5. API content terms**
- GIPHY and KLIPY: no caching or server rendering.
- Brandfetch free API: no server-side use.
- Pexels: credit required, no ML collection.
- Pixabay: 24-hour cache, no mass download.
- Freesound API: non-commercial without permission.
- Sonniss: no redistribution.
- Epidemic Sound: Safelisting needs partnership enablement.
- AssemblyAI and xAI: ToS bar benchmarking.
- Gemini API: users must be 18+, it is "not for consumer use", free-tier data trains models, and the EEA, UK and Switzerland require paid keys. This affects Google BYOK.
- Fable 5.1: requires 30-day data retention.

**6. Attribution**
- CC-BY-4.0: MFA English model, DNSMOS, Audiobox Aesthetics, pyannote.
- CC BY 4.0: Noto Animated Emoji.
- Pexels credit.
- Apple guideline 5.1.2(i): the consent screen must name every third-party AI provider that receives data.

**7. AI labelling**
- SynthID in Veo and Omni output survives re-rendering, so assume detection. Flag realistic generated scenes for disclosure.

**8. Current assets not to carry forward**
- SoundHelix and codeskulptor music beds.
- SFX files with no licence note.
- The Matter font (commercial, no licence in the repo).

### Bake-offs that must run before lock-in

Each of these is chosen by an eval, not by assertion:
- **ASR:** voter pair and cut-edge error.
- **Director:** Fable 5.1 vs Opus 5.5 vs GPT-6 Astra.
- **Watcher:** Gemini 3.8 Flash in agentic mode vs 3.1 Pro.
- **Colour:** tone-map operator.
- **Voice:** default voice-chain candidate.
- **Delivery:** −14 vs −16 LUFS; 1080×1920 vs 1440×2560 output.
- **B-roll sourcing:** stock hit rate on niche vertical queries.
- **Critic:** Gemini-watcher critic vs a frames-plus-metrics-only critic.
