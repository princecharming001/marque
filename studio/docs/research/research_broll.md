# B-roll craft, insertion styles, sourcing/licensing and retrieval/ranking for an agentic talking-head short-form editor (Yunicorn, Sept 2026)

## Summary
For talking-head shorts, b-roll should be optional and judged case by case, not a quota. Evidence from practitioners and data points the same way: the face carries trust. Good inserts are short (1–3 s full-screen, 0.5–1.5 s for memes), tied to a specific job, cut on the spoken word, graded to match the creator's footage, and kept off the hook and the punchlines. Only about 6% of 13.5M OpusClip clips use b-roll at all. The best quality comes from sending each beat to the right kind of asset: the creator's own footage or real screenshots and logos for claims; designed cards for abstract ideas; licensed stock or AI stills with parallax for mood; AI video only for a few hero shots. Four facts change the current plan. GIPHY and KLIPY terms do not license rendering their content into exported commercial videos. The Sora API is gone as of Sept 24, 2026, and Tenor's API shut down June 30. Qwen-Image-2.1 and FLUX.2 [dev] weights are non-commercial, and MiniMax H3's open weights exclude the US. Realistic AI b-roll triggers disclosure rules and auto-labels, which cut engagement about 7–8%. The strongest ranking stack runs in three stages: an open embedding model for recall, an open pointwise reranker, then Claude scoring a contact sheet against a rubric with a hard threshold. Refusing to insert anything must always be an allowed outcome.

## 1. When to cut away
Most rules here come from **vendor blogs with no data**: [AutoClip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/b-roll-advanced-techniques-for-clippers) states them as principles, and [Pireel](https://pireel.com/en/blog/b-roll-and-graphics-for-talking-head-video) disclaims any benchmark. They are useful priors for the agent, not hard laws.
- **Stay on the face** when viewers need to judge confidence, emotion, sincerity or personal experience ([Pireel](https://pireel.com/en/blog/b-roll-and-graphics-for-talking-head-video)). Never cut away on punchlines or reveals.
- **Literal illustration** suits real objects, places and actions. **Numbers and lists should be designed graphics, not footage.** Generic "typing hands, city traffic, coffee pouring" standing in for a specific workflow reads as filler ([Pireel](https://pireel.com/en/blog/b-roll-and-graphics-for-talking-head-video)).
- **Seams:** a punch-in "keeps the viewer in the room", so try it before b-roll ([AutoClip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/b-roll-advanced-techniques-for-clippers)).
- **Avoid the first ~3 s**, where cutting away "reads as an ad". If retention is already "flat and healthy", b-roll "will make it worse" ([AutoClip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/b-roll-advanced-techniques-for-clippers)).
- **Base rate:** 6.0% of 13.5M OpusClip clips (733,762) used b-roll, and 0.04% used a visual hook. The data covers Jan–Mar 2026 organic clips, and the report doesn't say whether its results are correlational or causal ([OpusClip, Apr 2026](https://www.opus.pro/research/broll-visual-effects-short-form)).
- **The best causal evidence is from learning science. [new]** A meta-analysis of 42 studies found that interesting but *irrelevant* visuals and details reduce recall and transfer (g = −0.33) ([Sundararajan & Adesope 2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-020-09522-4)). That makes "wrong or decorative b-roll is worse than none" research-backed, not just taste.
- **[corrected]** Guo, Kim & Rubin 2014 was *observational*, not controlled: 6.9M edX sessions. It found talking-head-plus-slides more engaging than slides alone ([ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2556325.2566239)). The takeaway is to keep the face in the mix, not to avoid inserts.

## 2. Duration and density (practitioner priors)
| Insert | Hold | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Short-form cutaway | 1–3 s | [AutoClip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/b-roll-advanced-techniques-for-clippers) |
| Maximum before focus drifts | 3–5 s | [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/research/broll-visual-effects-short-form) |
| Meme or reaction | 0.5–1.5 s | internal doc |
- **Budget:** about 15–25% of runtime, weighted toward the middle; beyond about ⅓ it becomes a montage ([AutoClip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/b-roll-advanced-techniques-for-clippers)). Treat this as a genre dial.
- **Perceptual floor:** people detect a named target in pictures shown for 13–80 ms, and naming it first helps ([Potter et al. 2014](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13414-013-0605-z)). A 27 ms replication failed, so treat the exact threshold as contested. Flashes of 0.5–1 s are safe when the voice names the thing. Text-bearing inserts need reading time proportional to word count.
- **Never reuse a clip.** EditDuet tracks repetition as a quality metric (0.174 per sequence) ([EditDuet](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.10761v1)).

## 3. Insertion styles (9:16)
- **Full-screen hard cut** is the default for literal inserts and flashes.
- **Top/bottom split or PiP with the face kept** suits demos, comparisons and anything longer than about 3 s. Subjects should fill at least ⅓ of an inset.
- **Creator matted over a screenshot or UI** (TikTok green-screen grammar) suits commentary, news and product talk. Matting options: BiRefNet (MIT) plus SAM 2 (Apache-2.0); **[new]** Robust Video Matting, which is GPL-3.0, temporally stable and needs no background or trimap ([RVM](https://github.com/PeterL1n/RobustVideoMatting)); and SAM 3 under Meta's commercial-permitting SAM License ([Meta](https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/sam-3-segment-anything-with-concepts/)). GPL-3.0 has no network-use clause, so server-side use is fine. MatAnyone and MatAnyone 2 are NTU S-Lab licensed ([repo](https://github.com/pq-yang/MatAnyone2)), so avoid them.
- **Cards, screenshots and UI captures** carry evidence and numbers. Zoom in on or highlight the relevant line.
- **Ken Burns or 2.5D parallax** works on stills and AI images. Depth Anything V2 *Small* is Apache-2.0; Base, Large and Giant are CC-BY-NC ([repo](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2)).
- **Safe zones [corrected].** TikTok publishes no single pixel safe zone; commonly cited values are about 130 px top, 484 bottom and 140 right ([creamate](https://creamate.ai/en/blog/tiktok-safe-zone-guide)). Meta's unified Reels/Stories spec is stricter: 14% top (~270 px), 35% bottom (~672 px) and 6% sides ([behaviour.digital](https://behaviour.digital/post/meta-reels-safe-zone-14-top-35-bottom-6-sides-the-2026-official-guide)). For cross-posting, validate text-bearing inserts against the union of both: roughly y 270–1250 and x 65–940 at 1080×1920. Evidence is secondary.

## 4. Timing, continuity, conform
- **The voice never changes.** Mute stock audio and add low foley or room tone ([StringLabs](https://stringlabscreative.com/avoid-these-common-stock-footage-mistakes-that-make-videos-feel-cheap/)).
- **Enter on the audio beat at the keyword.** Landing off a speech break "feels amateur" ([AutoClip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/b-roll-advanced-techniques-for-clippers)). Pull the insert from within ±1.5 s of the keyword ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/research/broll-visual-effects-short-form)). A 1–3-frame J-lead is anecdotal ([Superdirector](https://superdirector.app/learn/transitions-cuts)). Keep whips under 0.4 s and use them rarely, and avoid dissolves.
- **Conform pass (expanded) [new].** Before judging taste, every insert must pass these steps:
  1. **Transfer function.** iPhone HLG A-roll mixed with BT.709 stock looks grey, washed out or brightness-mismatched unless it is tone-mapped. Use ffmpeg `libplacebo` (bt.2390) or `zscale tin=arib-std-b67` ([guide](https://dev.to/masonwritescode/ffmpeg-hdr-to-sdr-tone-mapping-that-doesnt-look-washed-out-2026-24ie)).
  2. **Frame rate.** 24/25/30 fps stock and 24 fps AI clips inside 60 fps A-roll need RIFE interpolation or a deliberate cadence ([Practical-RIFE, MIT](https://github.com/hzwer/Practical-RIFE)).
  3. **Grade match.** Transfer colour statistics from A-roll reference frames with MKL or histogram methods ([color-matcher, GPL-3.0](https://github.com/hahnec/color-matcher)), then unify grain and sharpness.
  4. **Resolution.** Upscale low-resolution or cropped sources with SeedVR2 (Apache-2.0). Its model card warns that it can oversharpen "720p AIGC videos" ([HF](https://huggingface.co/ByteDance-Seed/SeedVR2-3B)).
- **Anti-cheese rules:** ban clichés, write a visual brief, prefer observational and specific footage, keep one "camera world", and never slap one LUT on everything ([StringLabs](https://stringlabscreative.com/avoid-these-common-stock-footage-mistakes-that-make-videos-feel-cheap/)).
- **Licence-driven taste.** Pexels bans using recognisable people "in any immoral or illegal way" and bans implying endorsement ([Pexels ToS](https://www.pexels.com/terms-of-service/)). Never pair stock faces with negative narration. Reject any candidate carrying another platform's watermark: Instagram filters watermarked and reposted content out of recommendations ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)).

## 5. Sourcing and licensing (verified Sept 2026)
**Free stock**
- **Pexels:** 200 requests/h and 20k/month. Portrait, landscape and square orientation filters. A prominent Pexels link must appear wherever API results are shown ([API docs](https://www.pexels.com/api/documentation/)). The ToS bans automated collection "including... for machine learning" and bulk copying ([ToS](https://www.pexels.com/terms-of-service/)). Embedding candidates per query, with no persistent index, is the conservative reading. The ~150K video count is unverified.
- **Pixabay:** cache responses 24 h, no permanent hotlinking, 100 requests per 60 s, no video orientation filter, and no systematic mass downloads ([docs](https://pixabay.com/api/docs/)).
- **Coverr, Videvo and Mixkit:** attribution requirements and restricted clips apply, as the researcher noted; not re-verified here.

**Premium stock (the quality tier)**
- **Storyblocks API [corrected]:** 8M+ assets under a "Standard API" licence (web, social, paid ads) or an "Enhanced API" licence (adds broadcast and OTT). Every download is licensed, and pricing is by quote. Indemnity is not stated on the page ([Storyblocks](https://www.storyblocks.com/resources/business-solutions/api)).
- **Shutterstock:** `orientation=vertical`; licence via `POST /v2/videos/licenses` ([SDK](https://github.com/shutterstock/public-api-javascript-sdk/blob/master/docs/VideosApi.md)).
- **Adobe Stock API [new]:** searches video and licenses HD or 4K assets on users' behalf ([Adobe](https://developer.adobe.com/stock/docs/api/12-licensing-reference)).
- **Getty/iStock:** contract-based.

**Memes and GIFs: not licensed for exports**
- **Tenor:** decommissioned June 30, 2026 ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/06/30/google-tenor-api-gif-updates/)).
- **GIPHY:** "Do not cache media URLs or copies... unless... explicitly approved". No proxying, "Powered By GIPHY" attribution required, and beta keys allow 100 calls/h ([GIPHY docs](https://developers.giphy.com/docs/api/)).
- **KLIPY** (Jun 23, 2026): "You may not cache or store KLIPY Content". Its API grant covers letting users "access, select, and post" inside the app ([API terms](https://klipy.com/support/api-terms)). The user licence is "personal and non-commercial" with no copying or modifying ([ToS](https://klipy.com/support/terms-services)).
- **Implication:** server-side rendering of GIPHY or KLIPY media into exported, monetised MP4s is not covered, and that includes Yunicorn's current path. Get written approval, or make memes creator-supplied.

**Real entities**
- Nominative fair use covers identifying screenshots and logos ([INTA](https://www.inta.org/fact-sheets/fair-use-of-trademarks-intended-for-a-non-legal-audience/)).
- **[corrected] Brandfetch's free Logo API** allows 1M requests/month with no attribution. But logos must be hotlinked in HTML img tags, "programmatic access... is not permitted", caching needs a custom agreement, and licensing logos to end users is banned ([guidelines](https://docs.brandfetch.com/docs/logo-link/guidelines)). Baking logos into renders therefore needs a paid or custom deal, or official press-kit logos. Playwright screenshots of real sites (Apache-2.0) remain the cleanest evidence source.

**AI labels**
- An AI disclosure costs about 7–8% of likes (1,135,817 TikTok posts from 8,650 accounts, plus 8 preregistered experiments) ([JCR 2026](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/advance-article/doi/10.1093/jcr/ucag013/8672493)).

## 6. Retrieval and ranking
- **Competitors** (Submagic with Storyblocks, OpusClip with Pexels plus AI, Jupitrr with Getty, Descript's generated clips) publish no ranking method.
- **Recall:**
  - Qwen3-VL-Embedding-8B (Apache-2.0) scores 77.8 overall on MMEB-v2 and 58.7 on video retrieval, with 1.4k stars ([GitHub](https://github.com/qwenlm/qwen3-vl-embedding)).
  - PE-Core (Apache-2.0) beats SigLIP2 and InternVideo2. PE-AV was released Dec 16, 2025 ([repo](https://github.com/facebookresearch/perception_models)).
  - Gemini Embedding 2 led a tiny 20-video test ([Mixpeek](https://mixpeek.com/blog/video-embedding-benchmark-2026)).
- **Rerank:** Qwen3-VL-Reranker-8B scores 79.2 against 73.4 for its base embedder on the MMEB-v2 retrieval average.
- **Technical-quality gate [new]:** DOVER gives separate technical and aesthetic scores, which cheaply rejects blurry or compressed candidates before the Claude judge ([DOVER](https://github.com/VQAssessment/DOVER); check its S-Lab component licence).
- **Claude as judge:**
  - Sonnet 4.6 matched Gemini Embedding 2 on Flickr30k 25-way hard negatives (R@1 0.796 vs 0.804, p>0.25) but took 6,100–9,415 s per 1,000 queries ([arXiv 2608.11343](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11343)).
  - This is still-image evidence only. Claude's value is rubric judgment: correct subject, legibility at size, watermarks, people shown in a bad light, and style fit.
  - Score each candidate on its own against a threshold, rather than forcing a pick from a list.
- **Loop evidence:** EditDuet's editor-plus-critic loop was preferred 81.8–86.9% of the time over baselines. Its GPT-4o judge agreed with humans 80.6% of the time, against 78.7% agreement between humans. The study used 35 raters on documentary b-roll, so it is small and in a different domain ([EditDuet](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.10761v1)).

## Weak or missing evidence
- There is no controlled study of b-roll's effect on short-form retention. Duration and density rules come from vendor blogs.
- Safe-zone pixel values are secondary-sourced.
- The hit rate for vertical, niche stock queries per provider has never been measured.
- The OpusClip "28% faces" claim has no stated method.
- The GIPHY User ToS could not be re-fetched (403).
- Storyblocks indemnity figures remain unverified.

## Verified recommendations
- Make b-roll an optional layer that must be justified. Every `add_broll` op names its job (illustrate a real thing, show evidence, render a concept card, punchline, cover a seam, rescue a sagging middle) and gives a one-line reason. 'Punch-in' or 'no insert' is always allowed. Block inserts in roughly the first 1.5–3 s and on punchlines by default, as a prior the agent can override with a stated reason. Budget about 15–25% of runtime, set by a genre dial. Basis: a seductive-details meta-analysis (g=−0.33) plus practitioner consensus.
- Route each beat to an asset class, not a keyword. Real entities or claims go to the creator's own media, Playwright screenshots or real photos. Numbers and lists go to designed cards. Mood goes to conformed premium stock. Punchlines go to memes only once licences are cleared. Everything else goes to a stylized AI still with parallax. Photoreal AI video is limited to 1–2 flagged hero shots. For logos, use press kits or a paid Brandfetch or custom agreement: the free Logo API forbids server-side access and caching.
- Default to a 1–3 s full-screen hard cut (memes 0.5–1.5 s) that enters on the keyword's word boundary and exits by the end of the phrase. Keep the voice untouched and mute stock audio. Use layouts that keep the face (split screen, or the creator matted with RVM (GPL-3.0, server-side), BiRefNet+SAM 2 or SAM 3) for UI, evidence and anything over about 3 s. Never reuse a clip. Validate text-bearing inserts against the union of the TikTok and Meta safe zones, roughly y 270–1250 and x 65–940 at 1080×1920.
- Make the conform pass mandatory, and run it before the taste judge: (1) HDR/HLG to one transfer function via libplacebo/zscale, (2) frame-rate match via RIFE, (3) automatic grade match to A-roll reference frames (MKL or histogram), then grain and sharpness matching, (4) SeedVR2 upscaling for low-resolution sources (watch for oversharpening). Reject any clip that still fails a 'same camera world' check.
- For quality, upgrade the stock tier to Storyblocks (Standard or Enhanced API licence), Shutterstock (orientation=vertical) and/or Adobe Stock API. Keep Pexels and Pixabay as fallbacks, following their attribution, caching and no-mass-download rules. Embed candidates per query and build no persistent index of provider content.
- Treat GIPHY and KLIPY media as unlicensed for exported, monetised videos until written approval arrives. Until then, make memes opt-in or creator-supplied, never serve KLIPY movie or TV clips by default, and remove Tenor code paths.
- Generative stack. Video: Gemini Omni Flash (tops the silent image-to-video board; 9:16; can also edit or regrade near-miss stock clips up to 10 s) and Veo 3.1 for native 1080p/4K (only at 8 s, then trim). Images: GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2/Pro, FLUX.2 [pro] API, or klein 4B self-hosted. Avoid: Sora (API gone), Qwen-Image-2.1 and FLUX.2 [dev]/klein 9B weights (non-commercial), MiniMax H3 weights in the US/EU/UK/KR (outputs included), and Kling without written permission. HunyuanVideo 1.5 is allowed for US-only use.
- Assume photoreal AI b-roll will be labelled. YouTube and Meta auto-label C2PA-bearing content, and SynthID survives re-rendering. The label costs about 7–8% of likes (JCR 2026). Default to stylized or illustrative generation, tell the creator when a realistic generated scene needs disclosure, and never try to defeat detection.
- Retrieval pipeline: (1) Claude writes a visual brief and 5–10 cinematic query variants. (2) Fan out across providers with portrait filters, 50–200 candidates. (3) Embed 3–5 frames per candidate with PE-Core or Qwen3-VL-Embedding-8B. (4) Score each candidate with Qwen3-VL-Reranker-8B. (5) Drop technical rejects with DOVER. (6) Claude scores the top ~8 on a contact sheet against a rubric: correct subject, expected-false discriminators, subject at least ⅓ of the frame, no watermarks or platform logos, no recognisable person in a negative context, style fit. (7) Accept only above a threshold; otherwise refuse.
- Ask the creator for pickups (a 2–3 item shot list) when the best asset would be their own product, workspace or hands. Match uploads to cues with the same embedding stack. This carries no licensing, label or originality risk.
- Evaluate b-roll on its own track: a golden set of about 100 real takes with human pairwise preferences, tracking wrong-subject rate, relevance precision, coverage against budget, reuse, conform failures and safe-zone violations. Add an EditDuet-style critic before render, and show each insert's reason plus 2–3 alternates in the app.

## Missed items added
- Causal evidence that irrelevant visuals hurt: a meta-analysis of 42 studies found 'seductive details' (interesting but irrelevant images or anecdotes) lower recall and transfer, g = −0.33. This is the strongest research basis for requiring every insert to have a job. [Sundararajan & Adesope 2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-020-09522-4); [2025 multilevel update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-025-10099-z)
- HDR/HLG conform: iPhone A-roll is often HLG while stock is BT.709. Mixing them untreated makes inserts look grey, washed out or mismatched in brightness. The conform pass must pick one output transfer and tone-map, e.g. with ffmpeg libplacebo (bt.2390) or zscale with tin=arib-std-b67. [DEV guide](https://dev.to/masonwritescode/ffmpeg-hdr-to-sdr-tone-mapping-that-doesnt-look-washed-out-2026-24ie)
- Frame-rate conform: A-roll is often 60 fps, while stock is 24/25/30 and Omni Flash outputs 24 fps. RIFE interpolation (Practical-RIFE, MIT) or deliberate frame-blend avoids cadence judder. [Practical-RIFE](https://github.com/hzwer/Practical-RIFE)
- Automatic grade match: color-matcher (Reinhard, MKL and histogram methods) transfers the A-roll's colour statistics onto inserts. It is GPL-3.0, which is fine for server-side use but the licence should be noted. [color-matcher](https://github.com/hahnec/color-matcher)
- Technical-quality filter before the Claude judge: DOVER gives separate technical and aesthetic scores for video, which rejects blurry, over-compressed or low-light candidates cheaply. Licence mixes a standard LICENSE with an S-Lab file, so check it. [DOVER](https://github.com/VQAssessment/DOVER)
