# Style treatments: podcast clip, sales/UGC ad, founder/brand update

Load this file when the brief names one of these styles, or a blend. Triggers: diarization finds two or more speakers who each hold the floor; the video promotes a product, offer, code or link, or the creator is paid, gifted or on affiliate; or a founder or employee speaks for a company. It shifts the topic files' dials; mechanics stay in `cutting-and-pacing.md`, `broll.md`, `captions-and-text.md`, `music.md` and `sfx.md`. Any paid, gifted or affiliate relationship loads the compliance floor, whatever the style.

## Principles

1. **Each style protects a different source of trust.** *Why:* a podcast clip runs on the guest's credibility and a real exchange, an ad on first-hand use, a founder update on accountability [X].
2. **Lo-fi and human beats polished.** *Why:* self-recorded phone creative had an 84% likelihood of beating studio creative [A]. TikTok asks for "not overly polished" [A].
3. **A podcast clip must stand alone.** *Why:* feed viewers have not heard the episode; an unresolved "that" or "like I said" wastes the hook window [X].
4. **Picture follows the voice, a beat late.** *Why:* viewers watch the current speaker about 74% of the time, and their eyes move 300–700 ms *after* a new speaker starts, without anticipating [L]. A switch well before the first word shows a silent face.
5. **Never splice a speaker into a claim they did not make.** *Why:* the guest's name is on those words. A meaning-changing splice, or gap change, is a misquote [X].
6. **Keep conversational rhythm, laughs included.** *Why:* answer gaps average about 200 ms [L]. Zero gaps sound robotic, 1 s gaps dead. Gaps of 700 ms or more signal a "no" or reluctance [L], so shrinking one erases hesitation and stretching one invents it.
7. **In an ad the creator is the ad; the brand is a guest.** *Why:* direct-response ads with the brand on no more than 25% of runtime were 4.8× likelier to rank top-20% for purchase intent [A, thin survey]. A logo splashed across the opening frame does not work [A].
8. **Show the real product, in use, more than once.** *Why:* creator ads showing the product throughout had +89% recall and +47% purchase intent [A]. Stock or AI stand-ins break the testimony, and AI labels weaken the viewer's bond with the creator [L].
9. **Disclosure and claims are part of the edit.** *Why:* the FTC wants disclosures "unavoidable", readable by size, contrast, location and duration, and warns that end-of-video disclosures get missed [P]. The editor controls every one of those.
10. **For a founder, edit intensity follows the news.** *Why:* effects make accountability read as marketing [X]. Clean sound builds credibility: the same talk with poor audio got lower ratings for speaker and content [L].
11. **"None" is often right here.** *Why:* the measured DOAC clips run dry, with no music or SFX, and Hormozi's use zero b-roll and keep fillers [V].

## Defaults and ranges

Starting priors, not rules. Give a one-line reason whenever you leave a range. Upper bounds are ceilings, not targets, and zero is valid in every framing, insert, music and SFX cell.

**Style matrix.** Cells are [X] unless marked, derived from the evidence below and the topic files.

**Compliance floor.** Law and platform policy, not priors; never traded for retention. The Director flags these points to the creator and does not give legal advice.
- **Disclosure (US).** In the video itself, not only the description, and not only at the end, where it is likely to be missed. Seen and heard is clearer. A platform label alone may not suffice ([16 CFR 255.0](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.0), [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)).
- **Disclosure (UK).** "Ad", upfront, "at the very beginning". The ASA advises against "sp", "collab", "gifted" and "in partnership with"; a platform "Paid partnership" tag suffices only if upfront and prominent ([ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html)).
- **Platform labels.** TikTok's commercial-content toggle is mandatory for paid, gifted and affiliate content ([policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/bc-policy/en)); YouTube requires the paid-promotion box ([help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235)); Instagram has a Paid partnership label. The Director flags these; the creator sets them at upload.
- **Claims.** Testimonial claims need the brand's own substantiation, and an atypical result needs disclosure of what people generally get ([16 CFR 255.2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.2)). Testimonials from nonexistent people, including AI-generated ones, are banned ([FTC 2024](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)).
- **Ad copy.** Meta ads may not assert personal attributes, such as "Do you have diabetes?" ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes/)). TikTok ads may not call weight loss easy or guaranteed, and must target 18+ ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-weight-management)). TikTok branded content may not promote weight-loss products at all ([policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/bc-policy/en)).
- **Music.** TikTok tells businesses to use its Commercial Music Library (about 1M tracks) for all commercial activity; any other track needs a licence ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/commercial-music-library)).
- **Other people's podcasts.** Clipping one normally needs permission, which does not satisfy YouTube's reused-content rule: monetization needs significant original commentary, substantive modification, or added educational or entertainment value ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)).
- **Fundraising.** A founder video about a live raise may be general solicitation under Rule 506(b), so flag it for counsel ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/general-solicitation)).

## How to decide

1. **Classify, and allow blends.**
   - **Podcast:** two or more diarized speakers, each with at least about 15% of the talk. **Sales:** a product plus an offer, code or link, or a brief marked paid, gifted or affiliate. **Founder:** the speaker represents the company.
   - A founder selling their own product gets the founder voice plus sales compliance. A sponsor read inside a podcast clip is cut or treated as sales.
2. **Compliance pass, before the story cut.**
   - Protect the word IDs of spoken disclosures, the CTA, codes and prices.
   - Tag objective claims: numbers, "clinically", "#1", health and earnings.
   - Never put a claim, number or result in text unless the creator said it or the brief supplied it.
3. **Podcast clip.**
   - **Moment.** A complete exchange with a turn, a stake or a laugh, and a payoff at 55–85% of runtime. Start where nothing in the first 10 s is unresolved; otherwise add a banner of 7 words or fewer, or pick another moment.
   - **Hook.** Pull a line forward only if it survives out of context; otherwise open on the question.
   - **Framing.**
     - 4K or one phone per speaker: active-speaker crops, switching 0–300 ms after the new speaker's first word.
     - 1080p wide: a stacked split, because single crops would upscale 1.78×.
     - A short volley of real turns may switch turn by turn (DOAC once switched 4 times in ~3 s). If sub-2 s turns go on for more than a few seconds, use the split; sustained switching above 0.5 cuts/s is "fast" pacing.
     - Keep screen geography: each speaker keeps their half of a split, with look room on the side they face.
     - Never cut inside overlapping speech; hold until one voice has the floor.
     - Confirm switches with active-speaker detection; hold the split when confidence is low.
   - **Backchannels** ("mm-hm") get no switch. Use a 1–1.5 s reaction cutaway, speaker audio running under it, only when the reaction is the story.
   - **Audio.** Tighten turn gaps to 0–250 ms, filled with room tone. Leave reluctant gaps (700 ms or more) alone, and never create one. Keep long pauses when the hesitation *is* the answer, as DOAC's grief clip does. Level-match speakers within ±1.5 LU short-term (`voice-and-loudness.md`).
   - **Captions.**
     - A colour per speaker (guest white, host yellow) and a new page at every speaker change.
     - On grief or serious material: smaller, mixed case, no emphasis colour.
     - An off-camera questioner can become a short question card. In a split, captions sit at the seam, off both faces.
     - Name and credential appear once, on the guest's first line.
4. **Sales/UGC.**
   - **Beats.** Map hook, problem, demo, proof and CTA. The hook take needs the face by 2 s and "you" by 5 s, with fillers removed. On Meta, "you" must not be tied to a personal attribute such as a health condition.
   - **Inserts.** The creator's product pickups first (request 2–3), then real screenshots (reviews with permission), then a designed offer card. Never stock or AI for the product. Keep the face on the testimony line.
   - **Disclosure label.** Lead with "Ad", which fits both US and UK guidance. Show it from 0.0 s in the top band, below the top UI zone (about 14% on Meta), for at least 0.3 s per word plus 1 s. A small persistent "Ad" tag is a safe option. Keep the spoken disclosure too.
   - **Sound.** A cleared bed, always with a no-music export. SFX only on product moments.
   - **Variants.** 3–5 hook alternates over one body (TikTok suggests 3–5 creatives per ad group), a Meta variant with attribute-asserting lines rewritten, and a textless export for the brand's overlays.
5. **Founder/brand.**
   - Rate the news: launch, update or bad news.
   - Prefer one continuous take. Protect accountability words ("we got this wrong") and the pause before them.
   - Proof inserts only: the real dashboard, product, email or team.
   - No hype overlays, stock, or AI likeness of the founder.
   - On bad news, use the quiet caption style.
   - Offer one soft CTA, or none.
6. **Render, watch it on a phone, and ask the critic questions.** Revise only on a failed question. Then subtract: disable each insert, zoom, SFX and overlay in turn, and drop any whose absence the critic cannot feel.

## When to break it

- **A long podcast monologue** (a hold over about 12 s) may take angle or crop changes every 5–6 s, as DOAC does, instead of cuts to a silent listener. From 1080p, keep punch-ins under about 1.2× or leave the hold.
- **Switch up to about 200 ms early** when the incoming speaker visibly motivates the turn: an inhale, a head turn, a raised hand.
- **A 1080p source may take a single crop** if the phone-screen check shows no softness.
- **A podcast moment built on a prop or chart** earns an insert; DOAC's only b-roll (6.5% of one clip) sat on a named thing.
- **A signature greeting of 1 s or less can open an ad.** Peloton's creator opened with "Hi, besties"; greetings showed +112% recall. It is identity, not throat-clearing.
- **A result-first ad may open on the result.** Redken's five-minute hair fix used a quick before/after and ranked in the top 2% of TikTok ads for retention. Check category rules first.
- **Finance and health products** drop SFX and often music; credibility outranks recall.
- **A high-energy founder launch** may take sales pacing and a bed.
- **Bad news** gets no music, zoom, SFX, speed change or insert over the key line. After its May 2025 AI backlash, Duolingo posted a sarcastic brand video, then needed a CEO clarification ([summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duolingo)).

## Worked example

**A. UGC ad (paid, 52 s raw, medium energy).**

> w0–w18 "Hey guys, so I've been using this serum for, like, a month now and I wanted to tell you about it."
> w19–w36 "If your skin gets tight and dry by like three p.m., you need to hear this."
> w37–w90 demo: "Two drops after I wash my face… it sinks in, it's not sticky…"
> w91–w112 "My skin has literally never been this calm. It's clinically proven to cut redness by forty percent."
> w113–w123 "This is a paid partnership with Acme, by the way."
> w124–w138 "Code JESS twenty gets you twenty percent off, link's in my bio."

- **Opening.** Cut w0–w18: it is preamble, not a signature. Open on w19 at 0.2 s.
- **Disclosure.** The spoken disclosure (w113–w123) comes last, which fails "upfront". Protect it, add "Ad · Paid partnership with Acme" in the top band from 0.0 to 2.5 s (five words × 0.3 s + 1 s), flag the platform toggles, and offer a variant with w113–w123 moved to just after the hook.
- **Meta check.** "Tight and dry" skin is not a health condition, so the hook stays. Rewrite it if the brief targets a condition such as rosacea.
- **Claim.** "Clinically proven… forty percent" keeps its spoken words but gets no stat card. Flag it: "Confirm Acme can substantiate this, or cut w100–w112."
- **Inserts.** Product pickups on "two drops" (w38), the texture (w52) and the bottle (w84), 1.5–2.5 s each. Keep the face on w91–w99.
- **Timing and text.** Demo at 1.05×; gaps of 200–300 ms. "Acme" is spelled from the brief; the card "JESS20, 20% off" runs from w124 to the end.
- **Sound.** A cleared bed 18–20 LU under speech that lifts into the CTA, and one soft pop on the code card.
- **Output.** 29 s, four hook alternates, a no-music and a textless export.

**B. Podcast clip (4K wide shot; host Maya, guest Dr. Lee).**

> w0–w22 (Maya) "…and that's where you've pushed back on the whole self-care thing, right?"
> w23–w71 (Lee) "Most people think burnout is about working too much. It isn't. It's about working without control…"
> w72–w74 (Maya laughs) … w75–w140 (Lee) "…so rest is the wrong medicine."

- **Start.** "That" is unresolved, so start at w23. A banner, "Why rest doesn't fix burnout", exits at 5 s. "Dr. Lee, burnout researcher" appears on w23.
- **Framing.** Lee gets an active-speaker crop. Maya's laugh gets a 1.2 s reaction cutaway, cutting back to Lee on w75. Her "mm-hm" at w96 gets no switch and no caption page. Lee's long answer gets a crop change about every 6 s, never on the payoff line.
- **Gaps.** The 800 ms gap after Maya's laugh (w74→w75) becomes 200 ms. The 600 ms pause before "so rest is the wrong medicine" stays, because it sets up the payoff.
- **Captions.** Lee's are white and Maya's yellow. Three emphasis words: "control", "rest" and "wrong".
- **Sound and ending.** No music or SFX. The clip ends 0.4 s after "medicine", at 44 s.

## Anti-patterns

- Ping-pong cutting on every "yeah", cutting inside crosstalk, or cutting to the listener during the payoff.
- One caption colour in a fast exchange.
- Clips that open on "and that's why…", frankenbites that make a guest say something new, and gap trims that make a reluctant answer sound eager.
- Soft 1.78× upscaled crops; music under a serious conversation.
- A logo on frame 0, stock hands standing in for the product, and AI "customers".
- A disclosure left at the end or hidden under the UI, or overlays stating numbers nobody said.
- A trending song on a business post, and whooshes on every cut.
- A founder turned into an ad read: kinetic captions, zoom pulses, a hype bed, every flub scrubbed, "we're excited to announce" left in.

## Critic questions

1. Is the claim, question or stake clear within 3 s, with a face or the product visible?
2. Podcast: does every switch land at or just after the new speaker's first word, except deliberate reactions and motivated early switches?
3. Podcast: can you tell who is speaking from the captions alone?
4. Podcast: does the clip make sense without the episode?
5. Does every spliced sentence, and every resized gap, mean what the speaker meant?
6. Sales: is the real product shown in use at least twice?
7. If paid, gifted or affiliate, is a readable "Ad" label on screen from the start, clear of the UI?
8. Is every on-screen number, claim or offer something the creator said or the brief supplied?
9. Does the video end within about 0.5 s of the last word (or after a title card or held visual that shows something new), with the CTA intact?
10. Founder: does it sound like the founder rather than a scrubbed ad read?
11. Is every word intelligible over the music?
12. On bad news or sincere beats, is the key line free of music, SFX, zooms and inserts?
13. Could any insert, zoom, SFX or overlay go unmissed? Then remove it.
